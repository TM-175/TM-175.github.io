Deviate Autotrader

An execution system that places live orders and grades trades after settlement.

From estimates to orders

I extended Deviate’s probability models into a separate trading system. It ingests live market data, compares prices with my estimates, and converts model-derived opportunities into orders on Kalshi.

After settlement

I built and operated the bot and automated the process of grading its executed trades against settled outcomes. This gave me a record of how its decisions played out in the market.

Built with

Python, Kalshi API
