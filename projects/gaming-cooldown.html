Computer vision / Gaming

Gaming Cooldown

We built a tool to help kids notice when a game is getting under their skin and take a break.

Recognizing frustration

Our software used the laptop’s built-in sensors, including its camera, to look for signs of rising frustration during a game. We used computer vision and machine learning to recognize those moments while the child played.

A chance to cool down

When the software picked up on signs of frustration, it could warn the player or prompt a short break. Our goal was to help kids build the habit of stepping away to cool down before anger took over the game.

Built with

Laptop sensors, computer vision, machine learning
