Virtual reality / Hockey

IceVision

My team in the Pittsburgh Penguins Tech Academy built a virtual reality experience to bring hockey fans closer to the game.

From fan interest to season tickets

We designed IceVision for fans deciding whether to spend more of the season at the rink. We wanted the time they spent experiencing hockey in VR to get them excited about attending games in person. For the Penguins, the goal was to turn that interest into season-ticket sales.
