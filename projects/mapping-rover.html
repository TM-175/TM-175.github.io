Autonomous 3D Mapping Rover

A Build18 team project using LiDAR and computer vision to map indoor spaces.

Mapping together

For CMU’s 2026 Build18, I worked on a team building a rover that could map spaces that might be hazardous for a person to inspect. Our project combined LiDAR and computer vision.

Two views of a space

LiDAR-based simultaneous localization and mapping helped the rover track its position and map its surroundings. Computer vision supplied a second view of the environment through 3D reconstruction.

Built with

LiDAR, SLAM, computer vision, 3D reconstruction
